RG Routine Lab

Plan rhythmic gymnastics routines: choose body and apparatus difficulties, see provisional difficulty totals, and mark everything on your music.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email rgroutinelab@gmail.com. Please include your device (e.g. iPhone or Android phone model) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Good to know

  • Totals are provisional: the app helps you plan, but only judges give the real score.
  • Your routines are saved only on your device. To move them to another device, use Share and open the file there.
  • Element values follow the FIG Code of Points 2025–2028. For other levels, enter your own limits and values.
