RG Routine Lab

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

RG Routine Lab does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Who we are

RG Routine Lab is an app for rhythmic gymnastics gymnasts and coaches, made by Saito Works. Contact details are at the end of this page.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account or login, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not use the internet at all: it does not even ask for permission to connect to it.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your routines: names, notes, apparatus, level, the elements you choose, music times and floor locations.
  • Copies of music files you add to a routine, so they keep playing even if you move or delete the original.
  • A small setting that remembers you have seen the welcome screen.

We cannot see any of this. Deleting a routine deletes its music copy, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data, under your device's settings.

Files you choose

When you add music or import a routine, you pick the file yourself in your device's file picker. The app only reads the file you pick. It does not access your photos, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Sharing routines

Only when you tap Share, the app creates a routine file (and, if you choose, includes its music) and hands it to your device's share sheet. You decide where it goes: for example Messages, email or AirDrop. The app you send it with handles it under its own privacy policy. Files you receive from others are imported on your device.

Children

Many gymnasts are children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, if optional online features are added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
